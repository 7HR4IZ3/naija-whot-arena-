How to play

Rules without the noise.

A compact reference for new players and the table arguments that happen after the third draw card.

Back to play

Match the number or shape on the top card. If nothing is playable, draw according to the room settings.

2 for pick two, 5 for pick three, 8 for suspension, and Whot cards can be enabled or disabled by the host.

When you have one card left, call Whot. Missing the call can add a house-rule penalty.

Finish the round with no cards, then settle the table using the room's selected scoring format.

2 for pick two

Draw two cards. This room can allow stacking or make the next player take the penalty.

5 for pick three

Draw three cards when enabled. The host decides whether it can be answered.

8 suspension

The next player loses their turn. Keep it on or off per room.
